## 🏢 Company Bio

**SirmTech Technologies** is a forward-thinking software development company focused on building reliable, scalable, and user-friendly digital solutions. We specialize in developing systems that simplify operations, improve efficiency, and support digital transformation for educational institutions and businesses.

Our mission is to turn complex manual processes into seamless digital workflows through modern, secure, and well-designed technology.

## 🚀 Our Core Services

### 🏫 School Management Systems
Complete platforms for managing:
- Student information and admissions  
- Exams, grading, and report cards  
- Attendance tracking  
- Fee management and financial reporting  
- Staff and timetable management  

### 🌐 Website Development
Modern, responsive, and secure websites for:
- Schools and colleges  
- Businesses and startups  
- Organizations and NGOs  
- E-commerce platforms  

### 🧾 Point of Sale (POS) Systems
Smart retail solutions with:
- Sales and receipt processing

Tailor-made systems designed to solve specific operational challenges.
